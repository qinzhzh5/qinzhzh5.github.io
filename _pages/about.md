I am a first year PhD student from [Department of Earth Sciences](https://www.earthsciences.hku.hk/) at HKU, working under the supervison of Assco.Prof. [Man Hoi Lee](https://www.earthsciences.hku.hk/people/academic_staff/11/?back=e9bffab5d5010de30eab938df848afd1). I get my Master degree from [School of Physics and Astronomy](https://spa.sysu.edu.cn/zh-hans/page/25),[SYSU](https://www.sysu.edu.cn/sysuen/), under the supervison of [Assco.Prof. Shangfei Liu](https://spa.sysu.edu.cn/zh-hans/teacher/148). I also work with [Dr. Jeremy Smallwood](https://www.ou.edu/cas/physics-astronomy/people/directory/post-docs/jeremy-smallwood), focusing on debris disc around binary-planetary or multi-planetary systems.

My research interests include planetary system dynamics and computational astrophysics, with a focus on using simulations and theoretical approaches to explain observational phenomena in astrophysics.
