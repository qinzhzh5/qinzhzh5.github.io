I am a first-year PhD student in the [Department of Earth Sciences](https://www.earthsciences.hku.hk/) at the University of Hong Kong, working under the supervision of Professor [Man Hoi Lee](https://www.earthsciences.hku.hk/people/academic_staff/11/?back=e9bffab5d5010de30eab938df848afd1). I obtained my Bachelor's and Master’s degrees from the [School of Physics and Astronomy](https://spa.sysu.edu.cn/zh-hans/page/25) at Sun Yat-sen University, where I was supervised by Associate Professor [Shang-Fei Liu](https://spa.sysu.edu.cn/zh-hans/teacher/148), working on dynamics of planetary systems. I also collaborate with [Dr. Jeremy Smallwood](https://www.ou.edu/cas/physics-astronomy/people/directory/post-docs/jeremy-smallwood), focusing on the dynamics of debris discs in binary–planetary and multi-planetary systems.

My research interests lie in planetary system dynamics and computational astrophysics, with an emphasis on using numerical simulations and theoretical models to interpret and explain observational phenomena in astrophysics.
